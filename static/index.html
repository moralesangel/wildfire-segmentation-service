What happens under overload

Press Stress test and watch dropped climb while latency stays flat. That is the design: when the queue is full the service refuses the frame and says so, rather than accepting it and falling behind.

An unbounded queue would not make it faster. It would convert overload into staleness — every frame still processed, each one further behind reality. For a live feed that is worse than a gap.
